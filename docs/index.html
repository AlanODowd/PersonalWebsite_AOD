Research background

1. Introduction

Cognitive neuroscientist with 7+ years’ experience in experimental psychology and lifespan research. My research incorporates precisely controlled, scalable methods, a lifespan and individual differences perspective and bridges fundamental perceptual science with health research. I am passionate about conducting research that explores the relationship between behaviour and aspects of cognitive, physical and mental health across the lifespan in the general population. I also have a particular interest in exploring how both exteroceptive and interoceptive perception are related to broader physiology, such as cardiovascular and respiratory function.
